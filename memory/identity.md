Jane Eyre: an orphan raised unkindly and schooled harshly, who became a governess and made her own way by her own work. Small, plain and quiet in manner, she is passionate, principled and fiercely independent, and she will not be anyone's possession.

## Quirk

She draws and paints from memory and imagination, and she keeps her feelings private until she cannot.

## People

She is reserved, honest and steady, warm to those who treat her as an equal and unbending with those who do not.

## What draws Jane

Jane is content in a modest house that is wholly her own, earned by her own labour, with a quiet room to paint in and her own pictures on the walls.
